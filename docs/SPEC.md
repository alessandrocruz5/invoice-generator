# Invoice Generator — Build Spec

Oct 28, 2026 · @Alessandro Cruz

This is a free, public billing statement generator for Filipino freelancers, modelled on [invoice-generator.com](https://invoice-generator.com/). It gets withholding tax right and is meant to go out alongside the user's BIR invoice, not replace it. A Generic mode keeps it usable outside the Philippines. Everything runs in the browser: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Vercel, plus one vendored PDF library. Export this doc as Markdown, save it in the repo as `docs/SPEC.md`, and build in the order given in section 9.

## 1. Positioning, scope and compliance boundary

The product is a billing statement, meaning a payment request, for Filipino freelancers, and it gets withholding tax right. It works alongside a BIR invoice and must never present itself as one.

Tagline for the page: **"Billing statements for Filipino freelancers, with withholding tax done right. Works alongside your BIR invoice."**

**Who it's for**

| User | What they use it for | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registered freelancers and professionals | The payment request sent before or with the BIR invoice: withholding math, net amount payable, payment details and QR. The "BIR Invoice No." field links the two | Primary |
| Newly registered, waiting for ATP booklets | Billing clients until the printed invoices arrive | Primary |
| Freelancers with foreign clients | The USD commercial document the client expects. The BIR invoice stays in their own books | Primary |
| Unregistered individual contractors | A legitimate payment request. The footer says plainly what it isn't | Served, never targeted |
| Users outside the Philippines | Generic mode, which works like invoice-generator.com | Secondary |

&#91;Certain\] There is no BIR category of "individual contractor" exempt from registration. Self-employed individuals must register under NIRC Sec. 236 even below ₱250,000 a year ([Respicio & Co.](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/is-bir-registration-required-for-low-income-freelancers-philippines)). Marketing to the unregistered would pitch the tool as a workaround. That invites criticism and rules out later partnerships with Taxumo, Juan or accredited e-invoicing providers.

**What it is not**

- &#91;Certain\] **Not a BIR invoice.** Under RMC 77-2024, a billing statement issued for a sale without an invoice "is not considered as evidence of sales" and is "tantamount to failure to issue" one. The penalty under NIRC Sec. 264(a) is ₱1,000–₱50,000 and 2–4 years' imprisonment. A TIN on the document changes nothing; validity comes only from an ATP or a BIR-registered system ([Batas Natin, RMC 77-2024](https://batasnatin.com/laws/rmc-no-77-2024-clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-provided-under-rev); [Respicio & Co.](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/what-document-should-freelancers-or-service-providers-issue-for-sales-of-services)).
- &#91;Certain\] **No official-invoice mode in v1 or v1.x.** E-invoicing service providers need BIR accreditation under RMC 110-2026, which requires three years of 5 million transactions and 1,000 active users a year ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/philippines-accreditation-framework-e-invoicing-service-providers.html)). Registering as each user's CAS needs a server-side ledger and a filing by every user at their own RDO.
- **Banned wording.** Never use "BIR-compliant", "BIR-ready", "official receipt" or "official invoice" anywhere: UI, PDF, page copy, SEO tags or social posts.
- **Outside the Philippines.** \[Certain\] Domestic B2B invoices must be structured e-invoices in Belgium, Croatia, Poland, Greece and (for large and mid-size firms) France ([Fintua](https://fintua.com/blog/einvoicing-mandate-watch-list-2026)). Generic mode has one tax rate per invoice, so EU/UK sellers charging two VAT rates need two invoices ([VAT Directive Art. 226](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/112/article/226)). UK VAT sellers invoicing in a foreign currency type the GBP VAT figures into Notes ([Wise](https://wise.com/gb/blog/vat-invoice-guide-uk)).

**Non-goals for v1**

- Login, cloud storage, emailing, reminders, payment-status tracking
- Official BIR invoices of any kind: ATP print templates, CAS, e-invoicing
- Exchange rates or conversion
- QR codes generated by the tool, or QR codes with an embedded amount
- Per-line tax rates, tax-inclusive prices, recurring invoices, credit notes
- PDF text in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Arabic, Hebrew or Indic scripts. The bundled font covers Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and Vietnamese only (section 6)

**After v1, in priority order**

1. v1.1: Form 2307 tracker per client and quarter; quotes that convert to billing statements; client address book and item catalog.
2. v1.1: acknowledgment receipt stamped "THIS DOCUMENT IS NOT VALID FOR CLAIM OF INPUT TAX"; peso equivalent at a user-entered rate; amount in words; statement of account built from History.
3. v1.1: installable offline app (PWA) and sharing to Messenger, Viber and Gmail.
4. Later, and only after an RDO confirms that computer-filled loose-leaf forms are still allowed under RR 7-2024: a print-alignment template for users' own ATP loose-leaf invoices.
5. Watch list: the first accredited e-invoicing providers and any import API; a definitive reading of the micro-taxpayer exemption in RR 11-2025 Sec. 4; whether the BIR's EIS portal (launched 27 Oct 2026) accepts manual uploads from micro taxpayers.

## 2. Stack and deployment

Build it as plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript ES modules with no build step, plus one vendored library, [pdfmake](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pdfmake) (MIT), for the PDF download. I tested pdfmake 0.3 in Chromium under the section 8 security policy. The PDF downloaded directly with the right filename, ₱ rendered, and the console showed no errors.

| Option | Pros | Cons | Compared with what you already know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Plain HTML/CSS/JS + vendored pdfmake (chosen)** | No build step and no runtime `node_modules`; two library files committed to the repo; Vercel serves it as-is | The inline editor's DOM code is hand-written; the PDF is a second layout (a pdfmake document definition) beside the on-screen sheet | Same JavaScript you write in Node and React, minus JSX and hooks |
| B. Vite + React + TypeScript + pdfmake from npm | Your daily stack; components suit editable label/value pairs and item rows; types guard the money math | A build step and a dependency tree for a one-page tool | Fastest for you if v2 adds accounts or a client list |
| C. Next.js + server-side PDF (Vercel Function running headless Chromium) | One layout: the PDF is a render of the HTML sheet, like invoice-generator.com's own server | Invoice data leaves the browser; cold starts; function size and cost; heaviest option | React you know, plus server PDF rendering you would have to run |

The objective pick is **A**. Based on your experience, pick **B** if accounts or a client list are coming soon. **C** is the only route to a PDF that is pixel-identical to the screen, but it breaks the promise that nothing leaves the browser.

Rejected for the PDF: `window.print()` opens a print dialog, whereas invoice-generator.com users expect Download to produce a file. html2pdf/html2canvas turns text into images that are blurry and not searchable.

**Deployment rules for Claude Code**

- The site lives in `public/`. `vercel.json` sets `"framework": null` (the "Other" preset) and `"outputDirectory": "public"` ([Vercel docs](https://vercel.com/docs/project-configuration/vercel-json)).
- Vendor pdfmake: copy `build/pdfmake.min.js`, `build/vfs_fonts.js` and `LICENSE` into `public/vendor/pdfmake/`. Pin the newest 0.3.x release that is at least two weeks old (0.3.11 on 28 Oct 2026) and record the version and SHA-256 hashes in `README.md`.
- Load pdfmake only on the first Download click by appending same-origin `<script>` tags. It weighs about 1.6 MB (816 KB library plus 836 KB Roboto fonts), and lazy loading keeps the editor's first load small.
- `package.json` exists only for tests: `"private": true`, `"type": "module"`, one script `"test": "node --test"`, no dependencies and no `build` script.
- The editor UI uses the system font stack. No CDNs or third-party requests.
- Target browsers: Chrome/Edge 99+, Firefox 93+, Safari 15.4+, set by `Intl.supportedValuesOf('currency')` ([caniuse](https://caniuse.com/mdn-javascript_builtins_intl_supportedvaluesof_key_parameter_currency)).
- Deploy by connecting the GitHub repo to Vercel. Pushes to `main` go to production; PRs get preview URLs.

## 3. Tax modes, fields and data model

The sheet keeps invoice-generator.com's regions. A **tax mode** select in the sidebar switches between two Philippine billing-statement modes and a Generic mode. It sets the title, the tax rows and the identity fields.

**Tax modes**

| Mode | Default title | Seller TIN prints as | Tax rows | Other rows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PH – Non-VAT** (default) | BILLING STATEMENT | Non-VAT Reg. TIN 000-000-000-00000 | None. Optional "VAT-exempt sale" note for 8% filers, off by default | Discount, Less: Withholding Tax |
| PH – VAT-registered | BILLING STATEMENT | VAT Reg. TIN 000-000-000-00000 | VAT treatment: VATable 12% (default), Zero-rated sale, or VAT-exempt sale | Discount, Less: Withholding Tax |
| Generic | INVOICE | Custom fields only | Tax as %, flat or off, as on invoice-generator.com | Discount, Shipping, Withholding (optional) |

- New users start in PH – Non-VAT with PHP as the currency. The mode is saved per document and in the defaults.
- &#91;Certain\] A non-VAT seller whose document shows VAT owes 12% VAT with no input credit, a 50% surcharge and percentage tax ([Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-per-rr-no-7-2024-as-amended-by-rr-no-11-2024/)). PH – Non-VAT therefore never renders a VAT row, and switching to it removes any VAT row.
- Title presets: Billing Statement or Statement of Account in PH modes; Invoice, Tax Invoice or Billing Statement in Generic. Free text is still allowed. A PH-mode title containing "invoice" triggers a warning (section 7).
- Shipping exists only in Generic. Freelance services rarely ship, and dropping it in PH modes avoids the question of VAT and withholding on shipping charges.
- Billing statement numbers default to a `BS-` prefix in PH modes, so they never collide with the serial numbers on the user's BIR invoices.

**Item fields:** name (required, up to 200 characters); description (optional, multi-line, up to 1,000); quantity (above 0, up to 4 decimals, default 1); rate (0 or more, up to 4 decimals, excluding VAT); amount (computed, read-only).

**Editable labels.** Every label is an input with default text. The keys follow invoice-generator.com's API (`header`, `to_title`, `date_title`, `payment_terms_title`, `due_date_title`, `purchase_order_title`, `item_header`, `quantity_header`, `unit_cost_header`, `amount_header`, `subtotal_title`, `discounts_title`, `tax_title`, `shipping_title`, `total_title`, `amount_paid_title`, `balance_title`, `notes_title`, `terms_title`). Added keys:

- `client_tin_title` (TIN), `bir_invoice_title` (BIR Invoice No.), `payment_title` (Payment Details)
- `vat_title` (VAT 12%), `withholding_title` (Less: Withholding Tax), `net_payable_title` (Net Amount Payable)

The seller TIN prefix is not editable, because it must match the mode. Keep "Less: Withholding Tax" as the default: \[Certain\] since RR 6-2026 (effective 16 Oct 2026) invoices must show creditable withholding as a separate item with that wording ([Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/lets-talk-tax/a-new-bir-invoice-requirement-for-business-compliance/)), so the layout carries over to any future official mode.

## 4. Currency selector

Changing the currency changes only how numbers are labelled and formatted. The digits the user typed never change, and no exchange rate is ever fetched or applied.

**Source of the list**

- Build the list from `Intl.supportedValuesOf('currency')`. Label each option with `Intl.DisplayNames(['en'], { type: 'currency' })`, for example "PHP — Philippine Peso".
- Render a native `<select>` with two `<optgroup>`s. **Common:** PHP, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD, HKD, JPY, CNY, INR, AED, NZD, CHF. **All currencies:** everything else, A to Z. Native type-ahead is enough; no custom combobox.
- If `Intl.supportedValuesOf` is missing, fall back to a hard-coded array of the Common group plus a few dozen more codes in `currency.js`.
- Default currency: the last one used, else PHP.

**Formatting**

- Format every money value with `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { style: 'currency', currency: code, currencyDisplay })`.
- Display style toggle: **Symbol** (₱1,500.00, the default) or **Code** (PHP 1,500.00).
- Symbols are ambiguous ($ is used by USD, CAD, AUD, SGD and others). The sheet and PDF therefore always append the ISO code to the Total and Balance Due lines, whatever the display style: "Balance Due (PHP)". The suffix sits outside the editable label so users cannot delete it by accident. In the PDF, a symbol the bundled font cannot draw (for example ฿, ₴, ₸, ￥) falls back to the ISO code (section 6).
- Number format: a select with **Browser default** (`navigator.language`) plus en-US, en-GB, en-PH, de-DE, fr-FR, es-ES, ja-JP and en-IN. This controls separators only, never labels or language.

**Decimal places (minor units)**

- Read them from `new Intl.NumberFormat('en', { style: 'currency', currency: code }).resolvedOptions().maximumFractionDigits`. JPY and KRW have 0, BHD/KWD/OMR have 3, most others have 2.
- Line totals, tax, and all totals round to that number of places (section 5).
- Unit prices may keep up to 4 decimals. Display them with at least the currency's places and at most the places the user typed.
- Switching to a currency with fewer places re-rounds totals. Show a one-line inline note, for example "JPY has no decimal places. Totals are rounded to whole yen."

**Typing numbers**

- Inputs use `inputmode="decimal"`. The decimal mark follows the selected number format: `.` for en-US, `,` for de-DE (read it from `formatToParts`).
- Reject thousands separators with the message "Remove thousands separators." Never guess that "1,500" means 1.5, because that silently bills 1,000 times less.
- Store the canonical form with a `.` decimal mark.

Show "Changing currency doesn't convert amounts." as helper text under the selector.

## 5. Calculations

All money math runs on `BigInt` scaled integers in one pure module, `calc.js`, with no floats anywhere. Floats get invoices wrong: `(1.005).toFixed(2)` returns `"1.00"`, not `"1.01"`.

**Rules for every mode**

1. Rates exclude VAT and tax. Tax-inclusive pricing is out of scope.
2. Let `m` be the currency's decimal places (section 4). Every rounding step uses **round half up** to `m` places.
3. Amount = round(quantity × rate). Parse both at 4 decimals, multiply as integers, round once.
4. Subtotal = sum of amounts.
5. Discount: in % mode, round(subtotal × % / 100); in flat mode, the typed amount (at most `m` decimals). A discount above the subtotal is an error.
6. **Net** = subtotal − discount. Net is the base for VAT, Generic tax and withholding.
7. Limits: 100 items, quantity up to 1,000,000, rate up to 1,000,000,000.

**By mode**

| Step | PH – Non-VAT | PH – VAT-registered | Generic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tax | None | VATable: round(net × 12%). Zero-rated or exempt: 0, labelled "Zero-rated sale" or "VAT-exempt sale" | % mode: round(net × % / 100), rate up to 3 decimals. Flat: typed amount. Off: 0 |
| Shipping | Not available | Not available | Flat; not taxed and not withheld |
| Total | Net | Net + VAT | Net + tax + shipping |
| Withholding | round(net × rate / 100) | round(net × rate / 100), on the amount before VAT | Same as PH, optional |
| Net amount payable | Total − withholding | Total − withholding | Total − withholding |
| Balance due | Net payable − amount paid | Same | Same |

&#91;Certain\] Expanded withholding tax is computed on the amount before VAT ([HitPay](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/withholding-tax-philippines)). Amount paid above the net amount payable is an error and blocks download. \[Guessing\] invoice-generator.com does not document whether it taxes after discount; Generic mode taxes after discount.

## 6. Numbering, dates, saving and PDF output

"Download PDF" builds the PDF in the browser with pdfmake, saves it as a file and records the document in History. Nothing is uploaded.

**Invoice numbering**

- The number is free text. The app cannot guarantee uniqueness across devices, and the field's tooltip says so.
- On download, store the number as `lastNumber`.
- "New document" suggests the next number by incrementing the last run of digits and keeping zero padding: `BS-0009` becomes `BS-0010`, `2026-099` becomes `2026-100`. A number with no digits leaves the field blank. Billing statement numbers are separate from BIR invoice serials; the BIR serial goes in BIR Invoice No.
- Warn, without blocking, when the number already exists in History.

**Dates**

- Store dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` strings from `<input type="date">`. Date defaults to today; Due Date starts empty.
- If Payment Terms matches `net\s*(\d+)` (case-insensitive) and Due Date is empty, fill Due Date with Date + N days. The user can still change it.
- Never call `new Date('2026-10-28')`: it parses as UTC and shows the previous day west of Greenwich. Build with `Date.UTC(y, m - 1, d)` and format with `timeZone: 'UTC'`.
- Print dates with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: 'medium' })` ("Oct 28, 2026" or "28 Oct 2026"). Month names avoid the 03/04 ambiguity.

**Saving (localStorage, every access in try/catch)**

| Key | Holds | Written when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `invoicegen:v1:draft` | The current document (section 3 shape) | Every change, debounced 500 ms |
| `invoicegen:v1:defaults` | Mode, From, seller TIN, logo, payment details and QR image, custom fields, currency, settings, labels, notes, terms, withholding preset, footer toggles | "Save as default" in the sidebar |
| `invoicegen:v1:history` | Up to 50 downloaded documents, newest first, without logo or QR images | Each download. The same number replaces its old entry |
| `invoicegen:v1:lastNumber` | Last downloaded number | Each download |

- **History** opens in a native `<dialog>` with columns for number, BIR Invoice No., first line of Bill To, date and balance due. Each row has Open, Duplicate and Delete, and the dialog has Clear history. Entries drop the logo and QR images to stay under the roughly 5 MB storage quota; opening one uses the current defaults' images, and the dialog says so.
- **Export history as CSV** in the History dialog. Columns: number, BIR Invoice No., date, client, client TIN, currency, subtotal, discount, VAT or tax, withholding, net amount payable, amount paid, balance due. Freelancers use it for their own books and for chasing Form 2307s.
- **New document** asks for confirmation, loads the saved defaults (or blanks), sets today's date and suggests the next number.
- **Export** downloads the current document as `billing-statement-{number}.json` (or `invoice-{number}.json` in Generic). **Import** accepts `.json` up to 1 MB, checks `version`, copies only known keys with type checks, and never uses `eval` or `innerHTML`. This is the only way to move documents between devices, so the sidebar says "Your data lives only in this browser. Export to keep a copy."
- If storage throws (private mode, blocked, or quota), keep working in memory and show one banner: "Your browser isn't saving this. Use Export to keep a copy."
- **Clear all data** (footer link) removes every `invoicegen:` key after confirmation.

**PDF generation (pdfmake)**

- `pdf.js` exports a pure `buildDocDefinition(state, totals, formatters)` that Node tests can call. The Download handler loads `vendor/pdfmake/pdfmake.min.js` and `vfs_fonts.js` once, then calls `pdfMake.createPdf(def).download(filename)`.
- Layout follows the sheet: logo left, title and number right; From with the seller TIN line; Bill To with the client TIN line beside the meta and custom fields; the items table; Notes, Terms and Payment details on the left of the totals.
- The payment QR image is fitted inside 120 × 120 pt, with its label above it, and never scaled up.
- In PH modes the footer line(s) print at the bottom of the last page, in small type, when switched on.
- Items table: `headerRows: 1` so the header repeats on each page, and `dontBreakRows: true`. The totals block is one unbreakable stack.
- Add a "Page X of Y" footer only when there is more than one page.
- Page size A4 or LETTER from settings. Default to Letter for en-US, en-CA, en-PH and es-MX browsers, A4 otherwise. Margins 40 pt.
- Filename `{Title} {number}.pdf`, for example `Billing Statement BS-0007.pdf`, with `/ \ : * ? " < > |` replaced by `-`.
- Logo: pass the PNG or JPEG data URL straight to pdfmake, fitted inside 150 × 60 pt.

**PDF font limits.** The bundled Roboto font covers Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and Vietnamese. I checked its glyph table against every symbol `Intl` produces:

- Covered: ₱ € £ ¥ ₹ ₩ ₫ ₪ ₺ ₽ ₦ ₼.
- Missing: ฿ (THB), ₴ (UAH), ₸ (KZT), ₾ (GEL), ₵ (GHS), ₭, ₮, ₲, ₡, ৳, the full-width ￥ used by ja-JP, and U+202F, the narrow space fr-FR uses as a thousands separator.
- Rule 1: before writing any money string, replace U+202F with U+00A0 and strip U+200E and U+200F.
- Rule 2: if a formatted amount still has a character outside the allowlist in `pdf-glyphs.js` (ASCII, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and B, Greek, Cyrillic, Vietnamese and the covered symbols above), re-format it with `currencyDisplay: 'code'`. THB prints as "THB 1,500.00".
- Rule 3: if user-typed text contains characters outside that allowlist, warn before download: "Some characters (like 日) won't appear in the PDF." Never block.

## 8. Public-use requirements

Nothing the user types may leave their browser, and the security headers enforce that rather than just promising it: `connect-src 'none'` makes outbound requests impossible.

**Security headers** (in `vercel.json`, applied to `/(.*)`)

  "framework": null,
  "outputDirectory": "public",
  "cleanUrls": true,
  "headers": [
    {
      "source": "/(.*)",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Content-Security-Policy", "value": "default-src 'none'; script-src 'self'; style-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data: blob:; font-src 'self'; connect-src 'none'; manifest-src 'self'; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'" },

        { "key": "Permissions-Policy", "value": "camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=(), payment=()" },

**Code rules that keep the CSP and XSS story true**

- No inline `<script>`, no inline `<style>` and no `style=""` attributes in HTML. Setting `element.style.*` from JavaScript is fine.
- Build the sheet with `createElement` and `textContent` only. `innerHTML` must never receive user data; enforce this with a test that searches the source for `innerHTML`.
- Render multi-line text with `white-space: pre-wrap`, not by converting to `<br>`.
- Show the logo only as `<img src="data:image/...">` after checking the MIME type and size.
- No cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from other domains in v1. pdfmake loads from `/vendor/pdfmake/` through same-origin `<script>` tags. Its two `new Function` calls sit inside try/catch, and it ran under this exact CSP in Chromium with no console errors.

**Disclaimer**

Show this text in the app footer and under Download PDF in the sidebar. It never prints on the document. The short PH-mode footer on the document (section 3) is separate, and the tool's own name or branding never appears on the user's document.

> Billing statements made here are payment requests, not BIR-registered invoices or official receipts. If you're BIR-registered, also issue your BIR invoice and enter its number in BIR Invoice No. This tool doesn't give tax advice. Outside the Philippines, check what your invoices must include; where e-invoicing is mandatory, a PDF may not count as a valid business invoice.

Privacy line, also in the footer: "Everything you type stays in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server."

**Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)**

- Labels on the sheet are editable, so each value input also gets a fixed `aria-label` with its default name, for example `aria-label="Bill To"`. Placeholders are examples, never the only label.
- Errors set `aria-invalid="true"` and link to their message through `aria-describedby`. The error summary takes focus and uses `role="alert"`.
- Every action is a real `<button>`, reachable by keyboard, with a visible focus ring. Icon buttons have labels, for example `aria-label="Remove item 3"`.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1. The sheet is a `<form aria-label="Invoice">`. Totals are not a live region.
- The app chrome may follow `prefers-color-scheme`. The sheet is always black on white.

**Public page basics**

- `<title>`: "Free Billing Statement Generator for Filipino Freelancers". The meta description should also mention invoices for clients abroad (Generic mode). Add a favicon and Open Graph title and description tags.
- Vercel already serves preview deployments with `noindex`, so only production is indexed ([Vercel KB](https://vercel.com/kb/guide/are-vercel-preview-deployment-indexed-by-search-engines)).

**Release rules**

## 11. Implementation decisions (2026-10-29)

These refine the sections above. Where they differ, this section wins.

- **Base branch.** Work merges into `develop` (Vercel preview). Production is one `develop` → `main` merge after the section 10 checklist passes.
- **Banned wording.** "BIR-compliant", "BIR-ready", "official receipt" and "official invoice" may appear only inside the approved disclaimer sentences exported from `public/js/copy.js`. `tests/wording.test.js` removes those sentences, then scans `public/` (except `vendor/`) for the phrases.
- **Donation.** A `support.html` page shows the owner's GCash/Maya QR image (`public/img/donate-qr.png`), linked from the footer.
- **Document shape additions.** `withholding_preset` (preset id or `"custom"`, default `""`); label key `ship_to_title` ("Ship To"). `fields.discounts` and `fields.tax` are `false` (off), `"%"` (percent) or `true` (flat), as in invoice-generator.com's API.
- **Numbers.** State holds canonical `.`-decimal strings only. Text that fails to parse stays in the editor's memory with its error, is never saved, and counts as 0 until fixed.
- **Flat amounts** (flat discount, flat tax, shipping, amount paid) accept up to 4 decimals and round half up to the currency's places, so changing currency never makes them invalid. Discount % accepts up to 3 decimals.
- **Negative intermediates** (reachable only with input that validation blocks) round half away from zero.
- **Paper default** follows the browser language when a document is created: Letter for en-US, en-CA, en-PH and es-MX, otherwise A4.
- **PDF filename** uses the title in title case: `Billing Statement BS-0007.pdf`.
- **History CSV** is UTF-8 with a BOM and CRLF line endings; text cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return are prefixed with `'`.
- **Extra test files.** `tests/withholding.test.js`, `tests/state.test.js` and `tests/pdf-glyphs.test.js` exist beside the section 9 list.
- **Browser floor.** Chrome/Edge 99+, **Firefox 98+** (native `<dialog>`, which section 6 requires; this also covers `structuredClone` and `crypto.randomUUID`), Safari 15.4+.
- **Not-saving banner.** It appears only when storage actually fails. Current browsers give private windows working (temporary) storage, so the section 10 check is done with site data blocked for the origin.
- **Previews.** The Vercel project's production branch is `main`. Preview checks need Deployment Protection turned off for previews, or a Protection Bypass for Automation secret sent as the `x-vercel-protection-bypass` header.
