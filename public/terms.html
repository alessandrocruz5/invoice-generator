This is not a BIR invoice

A billing statement is a payment request. Under RMC 77-2024, a billing statement issued for a sale without an invoice is not considered evidence of sales, and the penalty under NIRC Sec. 264(a) applies to failing to issue an invoice. A document is valid as an invoice only if it comes from an Authority to Print or a BIR-registered system, and a TIN on the document doesn't change that.

If you are BIR-registered, also issue your BIR invoice and enter its number in BIR Invoice No.

Sources: Batas Natin, RMC 77-2024 and Respicio & Co.

Outside the Philippines

Some countries now require structured e-invoices. Where e-invoicing is mandatory, a PDF made here may not count as a valid invoice. Check the rules that apply to you.
